01

Introduction

GLOCLIEN SYSTEMS LTD ("we", "our" or "us") recognizes the importance of privacy and responsible handling of personal information.

This Privacy Policy describes the information associated with our corporate website, how such information may be used, and the privacy choices available to website visitors and individuals communicating with our organization.

Our business activities include mobile application development, custom software development, systems integration, and IT consulting and support.

02

Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to our corporate website located at glocliensystemsltd.online and personal information provided through our published business email address.

Separate applications, software products, client systems and services may be governed by additional privacy notices and contractual arrangements.

03

Information We Collect

Our corporate website is an informational website and does not currently contain registration forms, payment forms or website user accounts.

Information associated with website visits and business communications may include:

  • Names and email addresses voluntarily supplied in correspondence.
  • Business enquiries, support requests and related communication details.
  • Information voluntarily provided when discussing technology services.
  • IP addresses and technical connection information processed by hosting infrastructure.
  • Browser information, device information and website request logs processed by service providers.

Please avoid sending passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information through general business email.

04

How Information Is Used

Information received through our corporate website and business communication channels may be used to:

  • Respond to business enquiries and support requests.
  • Discuss requested software and technology services.
  • Communicate regarding business arrangements.
  • Maintain website availability and security.
  • Investigate technical problems and potential misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
05

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection laws require a legal basis for processing personal information, the relevant basis may include:

  • Consent, where freely given and required.
  • Responding to requests or taking steps connected with a contract.
  • Legitimate business interests, subject to applicable individual rights.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.

The applicable legal basis depends on the circumstances and purpose of the processing.

06

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The current corporate website source does not intentionally install advertising cookies, analytics cookies or tracking scripts.

Hosting providers, browser services and external websites may independently process technical information in accordance with their own privacy practices.

If advertising, analytics or additional tracking technologies are introduced on this website, the relevant disclosures and consent arrangements should be updated.

07

Information Sharing and Disclosure

Personal information may be processed by service providers supporting our website, business email and technical infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide requested services or respond to enquiries.
  • Maintain the security of information systems.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Respond to valid requests from competent authorities.
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal rights.
08

Data Retention

Retention of personal information depends on the purpose for which it is held, the nature of the relevant business relationship and applicable legal requirements.

Website hosting and email providers may maintain operational records and backups according to their own retention practices.

Individuals may contact us to request information about applicable retention arrangements or deletion of their information.

09

Data Security

Our corporate website uses HTTPS to encrypt connections between supported web browsers and the hosting service.

Appropriate information security practices are important for protecting personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Visitors should exercise appropriate care when communicating information online.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data protection legislation and individual circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Request data portability where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Submit a complaint to a competent data protection authority.

Privacy requests can be submitted using the contact information provided below. Requests may require reasonable identity verification to protect personal information against unauthorized disclosure.

Children's Privacy

Our corporate website provides general business and technology information. It is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Parents or guardians who have concerns about personal information supplied by a child may contact us using the email address provided in this policy.

Applications intended for younger audiences require separate privacy arrangements appropriate to their intended users and applicable legal requirements.

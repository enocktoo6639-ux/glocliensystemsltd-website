01

Introduction

This draft privacy notice has been prepared for GLOCLIEN SYSTEMS LTD to explain the handling of personal information associated with its corporate website.

The organization must review this notice against its actual data processing practices before adopting it as an effective policy.

02

Scope of This Policy

This notice is intended for visitors to glocliensystemsltd.online and individuals who communicate with the organization through its published business contact channels.

It does not automatically cover separate applications, products or third-party services, which may have their own privacy notices.

03

Information Collected

The current static website design does not contain registration forms, payment forms or user account functionality.

Information may nevertheless arise from website hosting and business communications, including:

  • Names and email addresses voluntarily provided in correspondence.
  • Information included in business enquiries.
  • IP addresses and technical connection information processed by hosting providers.
  • Browser, device and website request information recorded by infrastructure providers.
04

Use of Information

Depending on the organization's confirmed activities, relevant information may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Responding to business enquiries.
  • Providing requested information.
  • Maintaining website availability and security.
  • Investigating technical problems.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Applicable legal grounds and any additional purposes must be confirmed before this policy becomes effective.

05

Cookies and Local Storage

The current website source does not intentionally install analytics cookies, advertising cookies or tracking scripts.

Hosting providers, external websites and other infrastructure services may process technical information independently.

This section must be updated if analytics, advertising technologies or other tracking services are introduced.

06

Information Sharing

Website and correspondence information may be processed by service providers involved in hosting, communications or technical operations.

Information may also need to be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal obligation.

Actual recipients and sharing arrangements must be reviewed by the organization before publication.

07

Data Retention

Retention periods should reflect the purpose for which information is processed, applicable legal requirements and legitimate operational needs.

Specific retention periods and deletion procedures remain subject to confirmation by the organization.

08

Data Security

Appropriate security measures should be maintained to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. The organization must confirm its actual security arrangements before adopting this notice.

09

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Submitting a complaint to a competent data protection authority.

Applicable rights, responsible authorities and request procedures must be confirmed according to the organization's jurisdiction.

10

Children's Privacy

This corporate website is designed as a general business information resource and does not contain child-specific account registration functionality.

Any future services intended for children must have privacy arrangements appropriate to their audience and applicable law.

11

Third-Party Services

The website is being prepared for hosting through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information in connection with its infrastructure services.

Further information is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

External websites and services may have their own terms and privacy practices.

12

International Data Processing

Internet hosting and communication providers may operate infrastructure in different countries.

The organization must establish any relevant international data transfers and applicable safeguards before finalizing this policy.

13

Mobile Applications

This corporate website privacy notice is not a substitute for the privacy policy of any individual mobile application.

Where applications are developed or published, their privacy notices must accurately describe their specific practices, including:

  • Personal and device information collected.
  • Application permissions and their purposes.
  • Advertising and analytics services, where used.
  • Third-party software development kits.
  • Information sharing and retention practices.
  • Account and data deletion procedures, where applicable.

These disclosures should reflect the actual behavior of each application.

14

Policy Updates

This draft should be reviewed whenever website functionality, service providers, data handling practices or applicable requirements change.

Once approved by the organization, the final policy should identify its effective date and subsequent revision dates.
